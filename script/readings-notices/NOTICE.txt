Hatena Keyword
--------------

As COPYING-mecab-ipadic-neologd.txt says, most of the surfaces and readings in
NEologd come from Hatena's keyword list, which is Hatena's copyright. Hatena
allowed its use for hobby, academic and private purposes, asked that it be
credited, and asked to be contacted about commercial use:

    Hatena: https://hatena.co.jp/
    Hatena Keyword: http://developer.hatena.ne.jp/ja/documents/keyword/misc/catalog

SudachiDict also contains part of NEologd, as NOTICE-sudachidict.txt says.
